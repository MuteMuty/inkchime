Inkchime

Draw lines, hear music

Draw a line, then press play.

Height is pitch. Thickness is volume. Colour is the instrument.

Space plays and pauses. Ctrl or ⌘ Z undoes. Or Tab to the canvas and place notes with the arrows and Enter.

Draw with a mouse, finger or pen, or move a cursor over the grid with the arrow keys and press Enter or N to place a note. Left to right is time, height is pitch, the brush sets the volume and the colour is the instrument. Space plays and pauses. Ctrl or ⌘ Z undoes.
